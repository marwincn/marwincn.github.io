FAQ

What is Airing?

Airing is a media tracker for TV, movies, and anime. See what’s airing on a calendar, continue watching from where you left off, compare ratings from multiple sources, browse seasonal anime, and keep everything in your library.

How do I track a show or movie?

Search for a title, add it to your library, and mark your progress. Upcoming episodes and releases show up on your calendar.

What do the calendar views do?

  • Month — overview of the whole month
  • Week — denser weekly schedule
  • Timeline — chronological feed of what’s coming up

Why don’t times match my local schedule?

Airing uses release/air times from metadata providers. Set your device timezone correctly; if a title still looks wrong, email us the title and what you expected.

Can I import lists from other apps?

Import support is on our roadmap. Email us which service you use (e.g. Trakt, MyAnimeList) so we can prioritize.

The app language looks wrong

The App Store listing and default UI are English. If you still see mixed languages, tell us your device language and iOS version.

How do I delete my data / request privacy info?

Email us from the address tied to your Apple ID purchases or the email you used with us. We’ll help with data deletion and privacy requests. Details are in the privacy policy.

Something is broken or a title is missing

Please include: device model, iOS version, Airing version, and screenshots if possible. For missing titles, send the official name and year.
